# Risk Register

Each entry cites the committed control that mitigates it.

| # | Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation (committed control) | Residual Risk | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Prompt injection via customer narrative causes the agent to follow an embedded instruction instead of triaging the dispute | OWASP LLM01 (Prompt Injection) | Medium | High | Context quarantine wraps all narrative text as inert data before any LLM sees it (`src/context/isolate.py::quarantine_narrative`); every detection is logged (`logs/agent_actions.jsonl`, `input_guardrail_check` records). Verified: `data/sample_disputes/DSP-3001.json`'s narrative contains an injection attempt, and `traces/run_transcript_DSP-3001.json` shows the final decision was driven by the real fraud signals, not the injected instruction. | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
| R2 | Agent discloses another customer's account/dispute data | OWASP LLM06 (Sensitive Info Disclosure) / NIST AI RMF Govern-1 | Low | High | Input guardrail refuses any narrative referencing an account other than the authenticated one, in dash/underscore/spaced forms (`src/guardrails/input_guardrail.py`, regression-tested in `tests/test_guardrails.py`). Verified: `data/sample_disputes/DSP-3005.json` triggers the block; `traces/run_transcript_DSP-3005.json` shows the request was refused, not answered. | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
| R3 | Card number (PAN) or account number leaks into logs or a customer-facing answer in plaintext | Synthetic-Data Rule / NFR-05 | Low | High | `src/guardrails/masking.py::mask_sensitive_text` is applied to every logged tool-call result (`logs/tool_calls.jsonl`), every audit record (`logs/agent_actions.jsonl`), and the output guardrail masks the final rationale before it is returned (`src/guardrails/output_guardrail.py`). All MCP tool responses are pre-masked at the source (`mcp_server/server.py::mask_pan`/`mask_account`). | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
| R4 | Agent auto-approves a high-value or high-fraud-risk case without human review | NIST AI RMF Manage-2 | Medium | High | `src/nodes.py::decision` forces `escalate_to_human=True` whenever the transaction is high-value or the fraud score exceeds the configured threshold (`config/config.yaml: fraud_scoring`), independent of the model's own confidence. Verified: `traces/run_transcript_DSP-3001.json` (`escalate=True` on a $2,450 transaction). | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
| R5 | Agent guesses a dispute classification for an ambiguous or out-of-scope request instead of escalating | OWASP LLM09 (Overreliance) | Medium | Medium | `route_after_classification` sends any `ambiguous_or_out_of_scope` or low-confidence classification to `escalate_ambiguous` rather than continuing the pipeline (`src/nodes.py`, tested in `tests/test_routing.py`). Verified: `traces/run_transcript_DSP-3003.json`. | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
| R6 | The reflection loop runs indefinitely on a persistently low-confidence case | Reliability / availability | Low | Medium | Bounded retries (`config/config.yaml: reflection.max_retries`) force an exit to `force_escalate`; a second, independent safety net is LangGraph's own `recursion_limit`. Both are tested in `tests/test_loops.py`. | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
| R7 | A Gemini API failure (timeout, rate limit, outage) crashes the pipeline mid-dispute | Availability | Medium | Medium | Every Gemini call is wrapped in `tenacity` retries (`src/llm.py`) and every worker node catches remaining failures and falls back to a deterministic offline heuristic (`src/nodes.py`, `generation_mode="offline_stub"` in every schema) rather than raising. | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
| R8 | Secrets (Gemini API key) committed to the repository | Secrets hygiene | Low | High | `.env.example` documents the required variable with no value; `.gitignore` excludes `.env`; no key is hard-coded anywhere in `src/` or `config/`. | Low | Agentic AI Eng. |
