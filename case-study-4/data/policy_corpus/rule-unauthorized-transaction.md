### Clause C1: Scope
Applies when the cardholder states they did not authorize, participate
in, or benefit from a card-not-present transaction.

### Clause C2: Filing Requirements
The cardholder must confirm the card was in their possession and that
no known third party (e.g. family member) made the purchase. A signed
fraud affidavit is required within the filing deadline.

### Clause C3: Filing Deadline
120 calendar days from the transaction settlement date.

### Clause C4: Provisional Credit
A provisional credit may be issued while the investigation is open if
the claim is filed promptly and the account is in good standing.
