title: Documentation Requirements by Dispute Type
dispute_type: general
---
### Clause C1: Unauthorized Transaction
Signed fraud affidavit; confirmation the card was in the cardholder's
possession.

### Clause C2: Duplicate Charge
Both transaction records showing matching amount and merchant.

### Clause C3: Goods Not Received
Proof of non-delivery and evidence of a merchant contact attempt.

### Clause C4: Billing Error
Receipt or price list showing the correct, agreed amount.

### Clause C5: Subscription Cancelled
Cancellation confirmation predating the disputed charge.
