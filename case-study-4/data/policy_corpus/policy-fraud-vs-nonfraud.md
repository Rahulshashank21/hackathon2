### Clause C1: Fraud Indicators
Card-not-present channel, a new or recently reopened account, multiple
recent unauthorized-transaction disputes on the same account, and a
transaction amount inconsistent with the account's typical spend all
increase fraud likelihood.

### Clause C2: Non-Fraud Indicators
Duplicate charges, billing-amount errors, and cancelled-subscription
continuations are typically merchant-side processing errors, not
cardholder fraud, and do not require a fraud score investigation beyond
a routine check.

### Clause C3: Mixed Signals
Goods-not-received disputes are usually non-fraud but should still be
fraud-scored if the account has recent unauthorized-transaction history,
since a pattern of disputes across types can itself indicate friendly
fraud.
