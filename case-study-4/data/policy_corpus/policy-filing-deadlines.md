title: Time Limits for Filing a Dispute
dispute_type: general

### Clause C1: General Rule
Most network reason codes allow 120 calendar days from the transaction
(or expected-service) date to file a dispute.

### Clause C2: Shorter Deadlines
Cancelled-recurring-transaction disputes (MC-4841) must be filed within
90 days, since the cancellation date is independently verifiable and
delay increases risk of merchant compliance disputes.

### Clause C3: Late Filings
A dispute filed after the deadline cannot be submitted to the network
as a chargeback; the customer must instead be directed to the
merchant's own resolution channel, and the copilot should recommend
"deny" with that rationale rather than a chargeback it cannot actually
win.
