title: Network Rule VISA-12.5 - Incorrect Transaction Amount
dispute_type: billing_error
---
### Clause C1: Scope
Applies when the amount charged does not match the amount the
cardholder agreed to at the point of sale.

### Clause C2: Filing Requirements
A receipt, invoice, or price list showing the correct amount is
required.

### Clause C3: Filing Deadline
120 calendar days from the transaction date.

### Clause C4: Partial Chargebacks
Only the difference between the charged amount and the correct amount
may be charged back; the transaction is not reversed in full.
