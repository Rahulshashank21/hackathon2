dispute_type: goods_not_received
---
### Clause C1: Scope
Applies when the cardholder paid for goods or services that were never
delivered or performed by the expected date.

### Clause C2: Filing Requirements
The cardholder must confirm the expected delivery/performance date has
passed and that they attempted to resolve the issue with the merchant
first, where feasible.

### Clause C3: Filing Deadline
120 calendar days from the expected delivery date, not the transaction
date.

### Clause C4: Documentation
Proof of non-delivery (tracking status, merchant correspondence) is
required before a chargeback can be filed.
