### Clause C1: Scope
Applies when a recurring/subscription charge continues after the
cardholder cancelled the service with the merchant.

### Clause C2: Filing Requirements
The cardholder must provide the cancellation confirmation (date,
reference number, or correspondence) showing cancellation occurred
before the disputed charge.

### Clause C3: Filing Deadline
90 calendar days from the disputed transaction date — shorter than most
other reason codes because the cancellation date is usually well
documented.

### Clause C4: Repeat Occurrences
If charges continue after a chargeback is won, the merchant may be
subject to a compliance case in addition to further chargebacks.
