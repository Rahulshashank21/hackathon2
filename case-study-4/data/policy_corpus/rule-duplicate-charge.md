### Clause C1: Scope
Applies when the same transaction was processed and posted more than
once for the same amount, merchant, and approximate date.

### Clause C2: Filing Requirements
Both transaction records (or statement lines) must be provided,
showing matching amount and merchant with distinct transaction IDs.

### Clause C3: Filing Deadline
120 calendar days from the later of the two transaction dates.

### Clause C4: Resolution
Duplicate-processing disputes are typically resolved without escalation
to full investigation once both transaction records are confirmed to
match.
