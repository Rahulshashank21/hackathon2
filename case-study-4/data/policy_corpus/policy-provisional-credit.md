### Clause C1: Eligibility
A provisional credit may be issued for unauthorized-transaction and
goods-not-received disputes while an investigation is open, provided
the account is in good standing and the dispute was filed within the
applicable deadline.

### Clause C2: Amount and Timing
The provisional credit equals the disputed amount and is typically
issued within 10 business days of the dispute being opened.

### Clause C3: Reversal
If the investigation finds the transaction was valid, the provisional
credit is reversed and the cardholder is notified with the supporting
evidence.

### Clause C4: High-Value Exception
Provisional credit on disputes at or above the high-value threshold
requires a human analyst's sign-off before issuance rather than being
auto-approved.
